Frequently Asked Questions

What is HEIC format and why does my iPhone use it?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is Apple's default photo format since iOS 11. It uses modern HEVC video compression to store high-resolution photos in smaller file sizes than traditional JPEG, but it is not natively supported by most Windows PCs or websites.

Can I convert multiple HEIC photos at the same time?

Yes, FluxWebP Pro supports batch processing. Drag and drop any number of .heic or .heif files from your iPhone and download the entire batch as WebP or JPG in a single ZIP.
