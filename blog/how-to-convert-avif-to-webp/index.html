How to Convert AVIF to WebP

F

Published: 2026-11-10 6 min read ✓ Verified Technical Review
Table of Contents

While AVIF offers cutting-edge compression, its adoption across legacy mobile operating systems, social sharing previews, and email clients remains uneven. Converting AVIF to WebP gives you the perfect middle ground: dramatic byte savings and universal 97%+ global browser support.

Why WebP Remains the Safer Production Target

Older Android devices running pre-Android 12, legacy Safari on iOS 15 or earlier, and desktop software like Adobe Illustrator or older Figma plugins still stumble over AVIF files. Converting your AVIF assets to WebP guarantees that every single visitor sees your visual content without delay.
