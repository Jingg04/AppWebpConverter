FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much smaller is WebP compared to JPG?

WebP lossy images are on average 25% to 35% smaller than comparable JPEG images at equivalent structural similarity (SSIM) quality scores.

Does converting JPG to WebP reduce image quality?

At standard quality settings (75–85%), the visual difference is imperceptible to the human eye while significantly reducing download sizes.

Can I convert multiple JPG photos at once?

Yes, FluxWebP Pro supports parallel batch processing. You can drag and drop dozens of JPEG images and download all converted WebP files in a single ZIP archive.
