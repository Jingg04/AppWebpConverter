FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my transparent PNG background remain transparent in WebP?

Yes! WebP provides full 8-bit alpha channel transparency support identical to PNG-24, preserving translucent drop-shadows and cutout edges.

Should I use lossy or lossless WebP for PNG images?

For graphic icons, logos, and UI assets with sharp edges, lossless WebP provides perfect pixel replication with ~26% byte reduction. For complex illustrations, lossy WebP at 85% quality can reduce file size by up to 70%.
