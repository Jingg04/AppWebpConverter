100% Database-Free Architecture

FluxWebP Pro operates purely on Git-managed Markdown & JSON files.

No PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MySQL, Firebase, or Supabase databases are used. Content is authored into /content/blog/ and media resides in /public/blog-assets/, ensuring total ownership, zero ongoing database hosting costs, and 100% uptime resilience.

Import & Validate Content Package

Upload a previously exported content package. The system will validate all article schemas, check for duplicate slugs, and require your explicit approval before updating.

Server-Side Git Provider Publishing

To enable automated direct publishing without local downloads, configure the GITHUB_TOKEN environment variable in your Vercel project settings. The /api/admin/content endpoint will automatically commit approved article drafts directly to your repository.
