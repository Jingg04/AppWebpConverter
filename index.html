Universal Compatibility

Supported Image & Camera RAW Formats

FluxWebP automatically detects format types, parses multi-megabyte camera RAW previews, decodes Photoshop composites, and rasterizes vector SVGs completely inside your browser.

Camera RAW

Digital Cameras

Directly convert RAW photos from DSLR & mirrorless cameras with instant preview extraction and EXIF metadata preservation.

  • Sony: .arw, .srf, .sr2 (Alpha A7, A9, A1, A6000 series)
  • Canon: .cr2, .cr3, .crw (EOS, Rebel, R-system)
  • Nikon: .nef, .nrw (Z-mount & D-SLR series)
  • Adobe DNG: .dng (Leica, iPhone ProRAW, Google Pixel, DJI)
  • Fujifilm: .raf (X-Series, X-Trans, GFX medium format)
  • Olympus / OM: .orf, .ori (OM-D, PEN)
  • Panasonic Lumix: .rw2, .raw (GH & S series)
  • Pentax & Ricoh: .pef (K-series, GR)
  • Other Brands: Hasselblad (.3fr, .fff), Minolta (.mrw), Sigma (.x3f), Samsung (.srw)
Pro & Mobile

Design & Mobile

Decode multi-layer Photoshop designs and modern mobile capture formats directly on-device.

  • Adobe Photoshop: .psd, .psb (composite raster rendering)
  • Apple & Mobile: .heic, .heif, .hif (iPhone Live Photos & bursts)
  • Tagged Image: .tif, .tiff (multi-strip & uncompressed)
  • Textures & Gaming: .tga, .targa (uncompressed & RLE)
  • Scientific & Legacy: .ppm, .pgm, .pbm, .pnm, .hdr, .qoi
Web & Vector

Web & Standard

Lossless & lossy conversion for all web standards, including vector SVG auto-scaled up to 2400px.

  • Vector Graphics: .svg, .svgz (crisp auto-scaled rasterization)
  • Next-Gen Web: .avif, .webp (recompression & optimization)
  • Standard Web: .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .jfif
  • Animation & Legacy: .gif (frame capture), .bmp, .dib
  • Icons & Cursors: .ico, .cur

Developer API

Browser API included

Use the same client-side conversion engine from your own scripts. Seamlessly handles Sony ARW, Canon CR2/CR3, Nikon NEF, Adobe DNG, PSD, SVG, AVIF, HEIC, TIFF, PNG, and JPG in one line of code. 

// Convert any camera RAW or standard image file to WebP
const result = await window.FluxWebP.convertFile(file, {
  quality: 82,
  preserveMetadata: true // Preserves EXIF from JPEG and Camera RAW
});

console.log(result.blob, result.width, result.height, result.savedPercent);
