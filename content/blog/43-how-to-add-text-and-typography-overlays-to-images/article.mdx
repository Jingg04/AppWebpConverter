## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a gradient scrim and why is it better than a black box?

A scrim is a smooth semi-transparent gradient (e.g. from 60% black to 0% transparent) placed behind text. Unlike a harsh solid rectangle, a scrim subtly enhances contrast where text sits while leaving the rest of the photo completely unobstructed.

### What is the WCAG contrast requirement for text over images?

WCAG 2.1 AA mandates a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal body text and 3:1 for large display headlines (18pt+). Busy photographic backgrounds require scrims or solid backing to meet this threshold.

### How many fonts should I use on a single promotional image?

Limit yourself to two font families: one expressive display or serif typeface for the headline, and a clean, legible geometric sans-serif for secondary subheadings and call-to-actions.
