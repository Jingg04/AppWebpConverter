> **Summary:** Stop cutting off heads and key subjects on mobile screens. Discover how smart focal-point cropping and responsive art direction outperform naive scaling.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the problem with naive scaling on mobile devices?

When a wide 16:9 landscape photo is scaled down to fit a narrow mobile screen, the subject shrinks so much that facial expressions and micro-details become unreadable.

### What is responsive art direction?

Art direction means serving fundamentally different crops or compositions of an image depending on screen size—such as a wide landscape for desktop and a tight vertical portrait crop for mobile.

### How can I set focal points in CSS?

Use the <code>object-position</code> property (e.g. <code>object-position: 70% 30%;</code>) combined with <code>object-fit: cover</code> to lock the browser's viewport zoom onto the subject's face or product focal point.
