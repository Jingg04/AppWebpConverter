## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a color space or color gamut?

A color gamut is the complete range of colors that a specific device (camera sensor, monitor, printer) can capture, display, or print. It is mapped as a geometric triangle inside the universal CIE 1931 chromaticity diagram.

### How much wider is Display P3 compared to standard sRGB?

Display P3 features a color gamut that is approximately 25% larger than standard sRGB, displaying significantly more vibrant emerald greens, saturated oranges, and deep crimson reds on modern Apple and OLED screens.

### Why do untagged Display P3 photos look washed out on standard monitors?

Standard sRGB monitors interpret raw RGB numbers without understanding the wider color coordinate scale. A Display P3 coordinate representing ultra-vibrant red gets clamped or interpreted as a pale, washed-out orange on an sRGB display.
