# Standardizing Catalog Images: Uniform Backgrounds, Padded Margins, and Shadows

> **Summary:** Stop your product grid from looking chaotic and disjointed. Master catalog standardization: uniform bounding padding, identical white backdrops, and aligned shadows.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the recommended padding percentage for product cards?

A standard margin padding of 8% to 12% (e.g. 10% breathing room on all four sides) ensures products never touch card borders while maximizing visible product scale.

### Why should a ring and a sofa not occupy the exact same pixel volume?

Visual scale normalization is critical. While both items should fit inside the standardized bounding box, scaling a tiny pair of earrings to the exact same size as a 3-seater sofa creates confusing visual dissonance. Use category-specific scale multipliers.

### How do consistent drop shadows improve catalog cohesion?

When all products cast an identical subtle shadow at the same angle (e.g. 45 degrees, 15% opacity), the entire catalog feels like it was photographed in the same physical room, establishing immediate design harmony.
