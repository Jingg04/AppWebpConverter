> **Summary:** Stop losing sales to suppressed listings and sluggish product pages. Master the exact image standards for Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, and WooCommerce.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the single best resolution for Shopify product images?

2048×2048px (1:1 square) in WebP format. This resolution provides seamless hover-zoom capabilities on desktop while loading fast on mobile devices when compressed to ~120KB.

### What causes Amazon to suppress product listings due to images?

The main hero image must have a 100% pure white background (#FFFFFF RGB: 255,255,255), contain zero text or watermarks, fill at least 85% of the frame, and measure at least 1600px on the longest side for zoom.

### What aspect ratio does Etsy prefer for thumbnail cards?

Etsy uses a 4:3 landscape ratio (e.g. 2000×1500px or 2700×2025px) for its search results grid, though 1:1 squares are widely used inside product galleries.
