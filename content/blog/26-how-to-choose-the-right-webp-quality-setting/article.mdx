## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why shouldn't I set WebP quality to 100%?

Setting lossy WebP to 100% disables quantization but still applies lossy color transforms, producing files that are often 3× larger than an 85% WebP without any visible difference to the human eye. Use Lossless mode instead if zero data loss is required.
