## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the recommended size for a full-screen website hero image?

For 1080p and 1440p displays, 1920×1080px or 2560×1440px compressed in WebP (under 200KB) is the standard recommendation. For mobile screens, deliver an alternative 768×1024px or 800×1200px crop via the HTML picture element.

### What aspect ratio works best for blog post featured images?

16:9 (1200×675px or 1920×1080px) and 1.91:1 (1200×630px) are optimal because they map directly to Open Graph social sharing cards for Twitter, Facebook, and LinkedIn.

### Why shouldn't I upload original 4K or 6K camera photos directly?

A 6000×4000 camera photo averages 15MB to 25MB. Loading that over mobile networks tanks your Core Web Vitals (LCP) and consumes excessive mobile data. Resizing down to 1920px width saves up to 95% of data.
