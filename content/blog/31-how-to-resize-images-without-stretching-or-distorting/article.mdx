## Frequently Asked Questions

### What causes images to look stretched on websites?

Stretching occurs when width and height dimensions are changed independently without preserving the original proportional aspect ratio (Width ÷ Height). If an image has an inherent ratio of 16:9 and is forced into a 1:1 box without object-fit, pixels stretch unnaturally.

### How do I lock aspect ratio when resizing?

In FluxWebP Resizer, enable the 'Lock Aspect Ratio' link icon. When you adjust the width, the height automatically updates according to the exact pixel ratio formula.

### What is the difference between cropping and resizing?

Resizing scales the entire visual content to new dimensions, keeping all original image elements. Cropping cuts off unwanted perimeter areas to fit a different aspect ratio while keeping the remaining pixels uncompressed and undistorted.
