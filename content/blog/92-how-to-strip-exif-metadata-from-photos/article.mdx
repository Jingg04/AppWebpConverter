## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does converting to WebP in FluxWebP remove EXIF metadata automatically?

Yes. By default, FluxWebP extracts pure pixel buffers and re-encodes clean bitstreams, automatically discarding unneeded EXIF headers, GPS coordinates, and camera thumbnails.

### Does stripping EXIF metadata reduce photo quality?

Not at all. EXIF metadata is stored in non-pixel header chunks (APP1 markers in JPEG). Stripping metadata removes only text and coordinates; not a single pixel of visual data is touched.

### How do I turn off GPS location tagging on my phone camera entirely?

On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Camera → select 'Never'. On Android: Open the Camera app → Settings gear icon → toggle off 'Save Location' or 'Location tags'.
