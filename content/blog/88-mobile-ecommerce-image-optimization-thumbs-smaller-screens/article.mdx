## Frequently Asked Questions

### What percentage of e-commerce traffic is on mobile in 2026?

Globally, over 74% of retail e-commerce traffic and 68% of completed transactions occur on mobile smartphones. Designing for desktop first is a critical strategic error.

### What is the 'Thumb Zone' in mobile UX design?

The Thumb Zone is the comfortable natural arc that a user's thumb can easily reach while holding a phone with one hand. Interactive image swipe carousels and zoom taps must sit within this lower-to-middle screen zone.

### Why are vertical 4:5 or 3:4 product images dominating mobile commerce?

Smartphone viewports are vertical (9:16). A vertical 4:5 image occupies nearly 70% of the mobile screen height without horizontal side gutters, delivering massive visual impact compared to tiny wide images.
