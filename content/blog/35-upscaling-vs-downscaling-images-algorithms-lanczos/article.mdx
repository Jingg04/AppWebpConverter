> **Summary:** Explore the computer graphics algorithms behind image resizing. Compare Nearest Neighbor, Bilinear, Bicubic, and Lanczos-3 for downscaling and upscaling.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why shouldn't I use Nearest Neighbor for photo resizing?

Nearest Neighbor simply duplicates or drops adjacent pixels without calculating weighted averages. This produces severe jagged edges ('pixelation') on curves and diagonals. It is only suitable for retro pixel art.

### Which algorithm is best for web downscaling?

Lanczos-3 resampling is widely regarded as the gold standard for downscaling photos. By applying a sinc window function across an 8×8 kernel, it preserves micro-contrast and edge sharpness far better than Bilinear or Bicubic.

### What is the ringing artifact in Lanczos filtering?

Because Lanczos uses a sinc filter with negative lobes, high-contrast borders (such as dark text on a pure white background) can exhibit a faint halo or 'ringing' line if oversharpened.
