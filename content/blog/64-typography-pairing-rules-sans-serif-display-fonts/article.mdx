# Typography Pairing Rules: Combining Modern Sans, Serifs, and Display Fonts

> **Summary:** Stop pairing clashing fonts. Master the golden rules of typography pairing: contrast vs concord, pairing display headlines with neutral body sans, and tracking.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the #1 rule of font pairing?

Contrast is king, but conflict is fatal. Pair typefaces that are distinctly different (e.g. an expressive high-contrast editorial serif headline with an ultra-clean geometric sans-serif body). Never pair two fonts that look almost identical (like Helvetica and Arial).

### How many different fonts should be on a poster?

Stick to a maximum of two font families. You can establish rich hierarchy using different weights (Bold, Medium, Light) and styles (Italic) within those two families.

### What is tracking and why does it matter for headlines?

Tracking is the uniform spacing between all characters in a text block. Tight tracking works wonders for bold, punchy display headlines, while loose (open) tracking gives all-caps subheadings an airy, premium luxury feel.
