## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does doubling the width and height of an image quadruple its pixel count?

Digital images are two-dimensional pixel grids. Pixel count equals Width × Height. Doubling both dimensions multiplies the total pixel area by 2 × 2 = 4 (e.g. 1000×1000 = 1MP, while 2000×2000 = 4MP).
