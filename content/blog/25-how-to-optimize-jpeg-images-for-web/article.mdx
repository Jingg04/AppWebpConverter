## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does progressive JPEG feel faster than baseline JPEG?

Baseline JPEGs render strictly top-to-bottom like a window shade. Progressive JPEGs display an instant low-resolution full preview that progressively sharpens, making pages feel loaded in half the time.
