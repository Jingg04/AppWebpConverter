# Implementing Schema.org ImageObject and Structured Data for Google Rich Snippets

<VisualAsset id="va-73-1" type="hero" src="/public/blog-assets/73-implementing-schema-imageobject-structured-data-rich-snippets/hero.svg" alt="3D visual of Schema.org ImageObject structured data unlocking Google Licensable badge" />

> **Summary:** Give your images superpowers in search. Learn how to implement Schema.org ImageObject JSON-LD structured data to unlock Google Licensable badges and rich snippets.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the benefit of adding ImageObject structured data?

ImageObject JSON-LD communicates explicit semantic metadata directly to search engine bots: copyright holder, licensing terms, creator credits, and high-res dimensions. It qualifies your images for Google's 'Licensable' badge and rich snippet carousels.

### What is Google's Licensable image badge?

The Licensable badge appears on images in Google Images when the page provides <code>license</code> and <code>acquireLicensePage</code> structured data. It tells potential buyers where to legally purchase or license the photo.

### Should ImageObject be placed inline with HTML or in the head via JSON-LD?

Google strongly recommends JSON-LD placed in a <code>&lt;script type='application/ld+json'&gt;</code> block within the document head or body. It is clean, separate from UI markup, and easy for crawlers to parse.
