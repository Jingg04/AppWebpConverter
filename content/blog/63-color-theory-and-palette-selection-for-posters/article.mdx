## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the 60-30-10 color rule?

The 60-30-10 rule is a timeless interior and graphic design proportion: 60% Dominant Base Color (canvas/background), 30% Secondary Color (sub-headings, visual framing), and 10% High-Contrast Accent Color (call-to-action buttons, key headline words).

### Why should I avoid using pure saturated red and pure blue together?

Placing 100% saturated red directly next to 100% saturated blue causes an optical illusion known as chromostereopsis, where the human eye's lens struggles to focus on both wavelengths simultaneously, creating an uncomfortable vibrating edge.

### What color palette conveys luxury and exclusivity?

Deep midnight blacks, charcoal grays, or rich forest greens paired with warm champagne gold, muted bronze, or crisp alabaster white convey understated luxury.
