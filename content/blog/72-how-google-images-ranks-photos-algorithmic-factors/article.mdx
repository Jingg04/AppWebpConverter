## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Google use computer vision to analyze what is inside an image?

Yes. Google uses multimodal AI models (MUM, Gemini Vision, and Google Lens) to construct deep semantic feature vectors of images. Google recognizes faces, objects, landmarks, text inside images (OCR), and artistic styles without needing alt text.

### Does the ranking of the hosting webpage affect image rank?

Heavily. Google treats the overall authority, page speed, mobile friendliness, and topical relevance of the host webpage as primary ranking signals for its embedded images.

### Why does Google prefer original photography over stock photos?

Google's algorithms can identify duplicate images across the web via perceptual hashing. Pages featuring unique, original photography are prioritized for transactional and product queries over generic stock photos.
