## Frequently Asked Questions

### What opacity should a watermark have?

For client proofing and security, 30% to 50% opacity tiled across the image provides strong deterrence. For public portfolio display, 15% to 25% opacity placed in a corner balances copyright attribution with viewer appreciation.

### Can thieves crop out corner watermarks?

Yes. Small corner marks are vulnerable to simple cropping. For maximum protection against unauthorized redistribution, use a repeating diagonal tiled pattern or place the mark semi-transparently across the central focal point.
