> **Summary:** White background or transparent alpha? Compare marketplace compliance (Amazon, Google) with modern responsive e-commerce design and dynamic dark mode themes.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why doesn't Amazon allow transparent backgrounds?

Amazon's mobile apps and website use a rigid white design system. Transparent images can render with black, gray, or checkered backgrounds if unmanaged, creating inconsistent and chaotic search results.

### Why are transparent WebP images superior for modern D2C Shopify brands?

Transparent WebP cutouts seamlessly adapt to any theme background color, light/dark mode toggles, and promotional sale banners without needing multiple photo exports.

### How much file size do I save switching from PNG to transparent WebP?

WebP lossy compression with alpha transparency reduces file weight by 65% to 80% compared to 24-bit PNG, cutting a 1.2MB cutout down to just 220KB.
