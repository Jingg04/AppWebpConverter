# Interactive Product Zoom: High-Res Tiling vs Lightweight WebP Optimization

> **Summary:** Give customers the tactile thrill of inspecting fine details. Compare Deep Zoom multi-resolution tiling with high-efficiency 2048px WebP zoom architectures.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum resolution needed for a good hover zoom?

A minimum of 1600px on the longest side is required for basic 1.5x zoom. For high-fidelity 2.5x to 3x magnifying glass zoom, 2048px to 2560px is the industry gold standard.

### What is Deep Zoom image tiling?

Deep Zoom cuts a massive 50-megapixel master image into a pyramid of 256×256px tile blocks across multiple zoom levels. The browser only downloads the specific tiles under the user's cursor, saving bandwidth on gigantic gigapixel assets.

### Why is an optimized 2048px WebP file usually better than complex tiling for standard e-commerce?

Tile servers require complex infrastructure and dozens of HTTP requests. A single 2048px WebP compressed to 120KB downloads in under 80ms over modern networks, providing instant hover zoom with zero backend complexity.
