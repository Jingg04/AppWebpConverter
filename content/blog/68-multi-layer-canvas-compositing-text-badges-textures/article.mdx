## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the 'Multiply' blend mode work?

Multiply multiplies the color values of the top layer with the bottom layer (Result = Top × Bottom / 255). Pure white becomes completely transparent, making it the perfect mode for applying dark textures, shadows, and ink stains.

### How does the 'Screen' blend mode work?

Screen is the exact mathematical inverse of Multiply. Pure black becomes completely transparent, making Screen ideal for applying lens flares, lighting glows, and particle dust over dark backgrounds.

### How do you give a modern digital poster an authentic analog feel?

Overlay a subtle film grain or folded vintage paper texture on the topmost layer, set its blend mode to 'Overlay' or 'Soft Light', and reduce opacity to 12-18%.
