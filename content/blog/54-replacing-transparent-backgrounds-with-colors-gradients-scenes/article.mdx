> **Summary:** Removing the background is only half the battle. Discover how to composite subjects onto solid brand colors, rich gradients, and custom scenes with natural lighting harmony.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do composited subjects often look 'pasted on'?

Subjects look fake when lighting direction, color temperature, or focal depth clash between the foreground and background. If the subject was photographed under warm golden light but placed in a cool blue winter scene, the human brain instantly spots the mismatch.

### How do I create a professional studio portrait background?

Select a subtle radial gradient transitioning from medium slate blue (#1E293B) at the center to deep charcoal (#0F172A) at the perimeter. This creates a classic studio spotlight vignette.

### Can I blur the new background scene to create depth?

Yes. Applying an 8px to 15px Gaussian blur to the background layer simulates an expensive f/1.8 DSLR portrait lens, pulling visual focus directly to your subject.
