## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I force AI to leave empty space for my headline and dates?

Include explicit negative space keywords in your prompt: 'minimalist composition with abundant negative space in upper third for text overlay, clean uncluttered solid gradient backdrop, off-center focal subject, rule of thirds framing'.

### Why should I avoid having the AI generate the final text directly?

Diffusion models treat text as visual brushstrokes rather than typographic glyphs, frequently producing misspelled, garbled letters. The professional workflow is: generate pristine background art with AI, then overlay clean vector typography using HTML5 Canvas or FluxWebP Poster Studio.

### What are the most effective style descriptors for modern posters?

Keywords like 'Swiss International Typographic Style', 'Bauhaus geometric minimalism', 'cinematic 3D glassmorphism', 'editorial photography', and 'risograph print texture' yield far superior results than generic terms like 'cool poster'.
