## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does AVIF compress smaller than WebP?

Yes. AVIF generally achieves 10% to 20% smaller file sizes than WebP at comparable visual quality, particularly at very low bitrates and for complex textures.

### Why isn't AVIF used everywhere instead of WebP?

AVIF encoding requires substantially higher CPU cycles (often 5× to 10× slower than WebP) and older devices lack dedicated AV1 hardware decoders, which can increase battery drain on mobile phones.
