## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why must the bottom-right corner of YouTube thumbnails stay empty?

YouTube overlays the video duration timestamp badge (e.g. '12:45') directly on the bottom-right corner across desktop, mobile, and smart TVs. Placing text or key faces there guarantees they will be covered.

### What is the 'Squint Test' in digital graphic design?

Squint your eyes until your screen blurs. If you can still identify the primary subject and read the main 3-word hook, your thumbnail will succeed on small mobile smartphone screens. If it dissolves into a mushy blur, simplify.

### What are Apple Podcasts official cover art rules?

Apple Podcasts strictly requires square 1:1 artwork between 1400×1400px and 3000×3000px, RGB color space, 72 DPI, exported as JPEG or PNG with zero pixelation or blurry scaling.
