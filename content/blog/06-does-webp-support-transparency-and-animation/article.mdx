## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does animated WebP compare to legacy animated GIF?

GIF is limited to an archaic 256-color palette and 1-bit on/off transparency, causing harsh banding. Animated WebP supports 16.7 million 24-bit colors, smooth 8-bit alpha transparency, and is typically 50% to 64% smaller in file size.
