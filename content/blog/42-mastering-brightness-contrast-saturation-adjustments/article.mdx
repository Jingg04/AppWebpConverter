## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between brightness and exposure?

Brightness linearly shifts all pixel tonal values up or down equally across the 0-255 RGB range. Exposure scales pixel values proportionally based on incoming light simulation, affecting midtones more than deep blacks.

### Why do images look flat and washed out?

Images appear washed out when they lack sufficient contrast—meaning shadows are gray rather than rich black, and highlights are muted rather than crisp. Increasing contrast expands the dynamic range.

### What is the danger of pushing saturation too high?

Excessive saturation causes color clipping, where subtle skin tones and color gradations collapse into flat, fluorescent blocks of pure RGB primary colors.
