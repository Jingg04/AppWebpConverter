## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does WebP support transparent backgrounds just like PNG?

Yes. WebP features a dedicated 8-bit alpha channel that supports 256 levels of smooth semi-transparency for dropshadows, glass, and hair cutouts.

### Can I use lossy WebP with transparency?

Yes! This is WebP's superpower: you can encode photographic foregrounds using lossy VP8 while maintaining a lossless transparent background, cutting file size by 60–80% compared to PNG-24.
