## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do old cutout tools give people 'helmet hair'?

Old tools relied on edge detection and thresholding, which treats hair like a solid boundary. Fine individual flyaway hairs get chopped off, creating an unnatural, blunt helmet appearance. Modern neural matting generates continuous 8-bit alpha transparencies for every single strand.

### What is color spill and how is it corrected?

Color spill happens when light bounces off the background (e.g. green trees or blue walls) and reflects onto the subject's hair. FluxWebP uses color decontaminating shaders that neutralize background color cast while preserving hair tone.

### Can FluxWebP cut out semi-transparent wedding veils or glasses?

Yes. BiRefNet and high-precision matting models predict true fractional alpha values (e.g. 30% opacity for veil lace or tinted sunglasses), allowing new backgrounds to show through naturally.
