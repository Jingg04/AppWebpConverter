## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are Amazon's main image background requirements?

Amazon product search guidelines strictly require the primary main image to have a 100% pure white background (#FFFFFF RGB: 255, 255, 255) with no text, watermarks, or distracting props.

### Why should I export product cutouts as WebP instead of PNG?

WebP supports identical 8-bit alpha channel transparency as PNG but produces file sizes that are 60% to 75% smaller, dramatically speeding up catalog page load times.

### How do I prevent floating products from looking fake after background removal?

Add a subtle, realistic contact shadow beneath the product base. FluxWebP includes an automated contact shadow generator that grounds the item naturally on white or colored backdrops.
