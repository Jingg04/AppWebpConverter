> **Summary:** What to do when AI fails. Master manual touch-up brushes, contrast pre-processing, and alpha restoration for difficult reflections, glass, and low-contrast edges.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does AI struggle when the subject matches the background color?

Neural networks rely heavily on local chromatic and luminance contrast gradients to delineate boundaries. If a white shoe is photographed on a pure white bedsheet, pixel gradient values approach zero, confusing edge prediction.

### How do I cut out clear glass or perfume bottles?

Glass is challenging because background objects show through the transparent liquid. In FluxWebP, switch to 'Refinement Brush Mode' to manually paint back glass reflections, then set layer blend mode to 'Multiply' or 'Screen'.

### Can I manually paint back areas that were accidentally erased?

Yes. FluxWebP includes an interactive 'Restore' brush. Simply paint over the accidentally clipped zone to instantly recover original pixel data from the cached source buffer.
