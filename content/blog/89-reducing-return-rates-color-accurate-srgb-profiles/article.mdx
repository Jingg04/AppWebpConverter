> **Summary:** Stop losing profit to expensive customer returns. Discover how strict sRGB color profiling, 18% gray card calibration, and ICC tagging prevent color mismatch returns.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What percentage of online returns happen because color looked different in the photo?

E-commerce industry studies show that over 22% of product returns—particularly in apparel, cosmetics, and home furnishings—occur directly because 'the color of the item delivered did not match the website image'.

### What happens if you upload an Adobe RGB or ProPhoto RGB image to a website?

Most mobile browsers and unmanaged displays assume images are in the standard sRGB color space. When an Adobe RGB image is rendered as sRGB without color space conversion, colors collapse: vibrant reds turn into muddy rust, and blues look dull and desaturated.

### How do you achieve true white balance during a photoshoot?

Place an industry-standard 18% neutral gray card in the scene under your studio lights, snap a test photo, and use your camera's custom white balance tool (or color eyedropper in raw processing) to neutralize all ambient color casts.
