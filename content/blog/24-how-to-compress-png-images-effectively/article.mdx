## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between PNG-8 and PNG-24?

PNG-8 uses an indexed palette of up to 256 colors (like GIF), resulting in tiny file sizes ideal for flat logos. PNG-24 supports 16.7 million colors with an 8-bit alpha channel, essential for photographic cutouts.
