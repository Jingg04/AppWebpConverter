## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between semantic segmentation and alpha matting?

Semantic segmentation assigns a binary label (0 or 1) to each pixel, which creates jagged, blocky edges on fine details. Alpha matting calculates a continuous floating-point opacity value (0.0 to 1.0) for every pixel, preserving semi-transparent glass, smoke, and fine hair strands.

### Which neural network architectures lead in background removal?

Architectures like U-Net, MODNet, and state-of-the-art BiRefNet (Bilateral Reference Network) are industry leaders, combining high-resolution spatial feature extractors with deep semantic understanding.
