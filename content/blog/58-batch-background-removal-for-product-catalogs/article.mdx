## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many images can I process in a single batch?

With FluxWebP's multi-worker client engine, you can drop 50 to 100 images per batch. Processing runs continuously across your computer's available GPU/CPU threads without arbitrary file caps.

### Can I automatically apply pure white and a contact shadow across the entire batch?

Yes. In batch settings, select 'Output: Pure White (#FFFFFF)' and check 'Add Adaptive Contact Shadow'. Every product in the queue receives identical treatment for complete catalog uniformity.

### Are the original filenames preserved in the final ZIP download?

Yes. FluxWebP preserves your original SKU and file naming conventions, appending '-cutout' or '-white' according to your export configuration.
