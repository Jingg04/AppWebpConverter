## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you add a white border around a cutout for a sticker look?

By dilating the alpha channel boundary outward by 10 to 20 pixels and filling the expanded mask with solid white (#FFFFFF). In FluxWebP Poster Studio, toggle 'Sticker Outline' to generate this vector contour instantly.

### Can I use transparent WebP files in Canva and Figma?

Yes. Both Canva and Figma fully support modern WebP with full 8-bit alpha transparency. WebP files import faster and use less browser RAM than heavy uncompressed PNGs.

### What DPI is needed if I want to print stickers on physical paper?

Physical printing requires 300 DPI. A 3-inch sticker requires a cutout of at least 900×900 physical pixels to print crisp and vector-sharp.
