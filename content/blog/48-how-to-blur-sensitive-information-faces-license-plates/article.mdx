## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can blurred or pixelated text be reversed by AI?

Yes. Light Gaussian blur and standard pixelation (mosaics) preserve statistical color gradients that modern machine learning models (like Depix) can analyze to reconstruct original alphanumeric characters. For absolute security, use solid opaque black boxes.

### Why are transparent redaction overlays dangerous?

Some software applies a black box with 90% opacity rather than 100%. Pushing contrast in Photoshop easily reveals the text underneath. Always verify 100% solid opacity.
