> **Summary:** Stop having your social media banners and graphics awkwardly cropped. Master the latest 2026 pixel dimensions for Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, X, and Facebook.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the recommended Instagram post size in 2026?

Instagram strongly favors vertical 4:5 portraits (1080×1350px) because they occupy significantly more vertical screen real estate in the feed compared to square 1:1 (1080×1080px) or horizontal 1.91:1 (1080×566px).

### What resolution should YouTube thumbnails be?

YouTube thumbnails must be exactly 1280×720px (16:9 aspect ratio) with a minimum width of 640px. The file size must remain strictly under 2MB.

### Why do LinkedIn headers look blurry on desktop?

LinkedIn company banners are 1128×191px, while personal profile headers are 1584×396px. If you upload a heavily compressed JPG, text artifacts appear. Exporting a clean high-quality WebP or PNG prevents re-compression artifacts.
