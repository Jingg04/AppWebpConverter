## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why won't Chrome or Windows display my iPhone HEIC photo?

HEIC uses the proprietary HEVC (H.265) patent pool. Due to licensing royalties, Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, and stock Windows installations do not decode HEIC natively on the web.
