## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the single most important element on a flash sale banner?

The offer value proposition (e.g. 'UP TO 50% OFF EVERYTHING') paired with a high-contrast Call to Action (CTA) button. Shoppers must understand the discount instantly.

### Why should you avoid rotating hero carousels (sliders)?

Extensive UX research by Baymard Institute proves that auto-rotating carousels destroy conversions. Shoppers perceive them as ads, suffer banner blindness, and the slide they wanted to click rotates away right as they tap it. Use a single commanding static banner instead.

### What colors convert best for urgency badges?

High-contrast warm tones (vibrant crimson red, electric tangerine, energetic golden amber) trigger psychological urgency and FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) when set against dark or neutral backgrounds.
