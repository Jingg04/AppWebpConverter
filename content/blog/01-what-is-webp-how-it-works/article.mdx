> **Summary:** Understand how Google's WebP image format achieves 25% to 34% smaller file sizes than JPEG while preserving full transparency and animation support.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does WebP work in Apple Safari and iOS?

Yes. Apple added full native WebP decoding in iOS 14 and macOS Big Sur (Safari 14+), bringing WebP browser compatibility to over 97% of worldwide internet users.

### Can WebP replace both JPEG and PNG on my website?

Yes. WebP supports both lossy compression (ideal for photographs replacing JPEG) and lossless compression with 8-bit alpha transparency (ideal for logos, icons, and UI assets replacing PNG).

### Why is WebP significantly smaller than standard JPEG?

WebP uses intra-frame predictive coding borrowed from the VP8 video codec, predicting block pixel values from neighboring blocks and only storing the residual difference.
