## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will converting an existing JPEG to WebP degrade visual quality?

Because re-encoding between two lossy formats can introduce generational loss, keeping WebP quality at 82–85% or using near-lossless settings ensures visually identical results while shedding 20–30% of file size.

### Which format uses less CPU during mobile browser decoding?

Modern mobile chipsets (Apple Silicon, Snapdragon, MediaTek) include dedicated hardware-accelerated VP8/WebP decoders, making WebP decoding performance on par with traditional baseline JPEG.
