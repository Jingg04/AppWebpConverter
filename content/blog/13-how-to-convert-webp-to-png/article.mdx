## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do WebP files become larger when converted back to PNG?

PNG uses DEFLATE compression, which is mathematically less efficient than WebP's predictive coding. While the visual pixel quality remains identical, the uncompressed PNG bitstream naturally requires more bytes.
