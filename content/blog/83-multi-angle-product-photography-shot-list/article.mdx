## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the optimal number of images for an e-commerce listing?

Top-performing listings feature 5 to 7 distinct images. Research across Amazon and Shopify shows conversion rates peak at 6 photos, answering visual questions about size, texture, and real-world usage.

### Why is a 'Scale Shot' critical for reducing returns?

Customers are terrible at visualizing dimensions from written text (e.g. '12 x 8 x 4 inches'). Showing the product next to a universally recognized object (like a smartphone, water bottle, or held in a human hand) eliminates surprises and cuts return rates.

### What is the role of the Macro Close-Up shot?

A close-up shot focusing on stitching, fabric grain, metallic bevels, or organic ingredients provides tangible proof of craftsmanship and material quality, justifying premium pricing.
