# When Should You Keep an Image in PNG Instead of Converting It?

<VisualAsset id="va-8-1" type="hero" src="/public/blog-assets/08-when-to-keep-png-format/hero.svg" alt="Digital vault representing master PNG archival storage alongside fast WebP web distribution" />

> **Summary:** While WebP dominates web delivery, PNG remains essential for master source files, non-web desktop software, and raw asset editing pipelines. Learn when to keep PNG.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I keep PNG files on my local hard drive while serving WebP on the web?

Yes. The industry best practice is to maintain uncompressed PNG master assets in your repository or media archive, and generate optimized WebP files for web distribution.
