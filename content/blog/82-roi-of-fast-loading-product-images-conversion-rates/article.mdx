## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does Google's research say about mobile page speed and bounce rates?

Google research indicates that as page load time goes from 1 second to 3 seconds, the probability of a bounce increases by 32%. When load time reaches 5 seconds, bounce probability increases by 90%.

### How much revenue does a 100ms speed improvement generate?

A famous study by Deloitte and Google revealed that a mere 0.1-second improvement in mobile site speed yielded an 8.4% increase in conversions for retail sites and a 9.2% increase in average order value.

### Why are product gallery images the biggest culprit in cart abandonment?

Shoppers swipe through 4 to 8 product photos before clicking 'Add to Cart'. If swiping triggers loading spinners, buyer momentum is shattered, leading to immediate tab closure and cart abandonment.
