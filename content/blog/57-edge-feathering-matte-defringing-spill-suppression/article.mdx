# Edge Feathering, Matte Defringing, and Spill Suppression Techniques

> **Summary:** Say goodbye to embarrassing green halos and white edge fringes. Master post-segmentation edge refinement: matte contraction (choke), feathering, and spill suppression.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is color fringe or halo on an image cutout?

Color fringe occurs when pixels along the perimeter of the subject are partially mixed with the original background color during photography. When placed onto a new background, those border pixels form a noticeable, distracting halo.

### What does 'choking' a matte mean?

Choking (contracting) an alpha matte erodes the outer boundary by 1 or 2 pixels inward. This strips away contaminated border pixels before applying a soft fractional sub-pixel feather.

### How does mathematical green/blue spill suppression work?

Spill suppression clamps the offending color channel based on surrounding channels. For green spill: <code>G' = min(G, (R + B) / 2)</code>. This neutralizes green light bounce without altering natural skin and hair colors.
