## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between rotating and flipping an image?

Rotating turns an image around its central axis by a specific angle (e.g., 90° clockwise or counter-clockwise). Flipping mirrors the image across an axis: horizontal flipping reflects left-to-right (mirror image), while vertical flipping flips top-to-bottom.

### Does cropping an image reduce its file size?

Yes. Cropping discards unselected pixel rows and columns. Fewer total pixels directly translates into smaller encoded binary file size, especially when exported as WebP.

### Can I undo accidental crops or flips?

Yes. In FluxWebP Image Editor, all operations are tracked in a non-destructive session history stack. You can press Ctrl+Z (or Cmd+Z) to step backward at any time.
