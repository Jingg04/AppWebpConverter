## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the primary purpose of alt text?

Alt text serves two essential purposes: 1. Accessibility for visually impaired users who rely on screen readers, and 2. Providing search engine bots with semantic descriptions of image content so it can be indexed and ranked in Google Images.

### How should I name image files before uploading?

Use lowercase, hyphen-separated descriptive words that describe the image subject (e.g., <code>modern-scandinavian-dining-table.webp</code> instead of <code>IMG_8492.JPG</code>). Avoid keyword stuffing.

### Does Google penalize keyword stuffing in alt text?

Yes. Google's Search Essentials explicitly state that stuffing alt text with long lists of repetitive keywords results in a negative user experience and can cause the page to be penalized or excluded from Google Images.
