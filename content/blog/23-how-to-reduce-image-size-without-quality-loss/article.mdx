## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is chroma subsampling and how does it shrink images?

Chroma subsampling (like 4:2:0) halves color resolution while preserving full luminance (brightness) detail. Since the human eye has far fewer color receptors, this saves 50% of raw data invisibly.
