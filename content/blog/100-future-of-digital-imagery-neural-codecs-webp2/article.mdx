## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is WebP 2 and when will it arrive?

WebP 2 is Google's successor to WebP, designed to achieve comparable or superior compression efficiency to AVIF while significantly speeding up encoding times. It introduces improved directional intra-prediction and advanced adaptive quantization.

### What is a 'Learned Neural Codec'?

Unlike traditional mathematical codecs (DCT, discrete wavelet transforms), learned neural codecs use deep variational autoencoders (VAEs) trained on billions of images. They understand high-level semantic concepts, allowing them to reconstruct photorealistic textures at bitrates 5x to 10x lower than JPEG.

### How will Generative Super-Resolution change web delivery?

Instead of downloading a massive 4K photograph over cellular networks, websites will deliver a microscopic 500px base file (15KB). A client-side neural model running on your phone's NPU (Neural Processing Unit) reconstructs razor-sharp 4K textures in real-time.
