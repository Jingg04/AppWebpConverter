## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is sRGB the only safe color profile for the web?

Many cameras shoot in Adobe RGB or Display P3. While these have wider gamuts, older monitors and unmanaged browsers interpret them incorrectly, causing skin tones and vibrant colors to look washed-out and muddy. Converting to standard sRGB guarantees uniform color everywhere.

### Should I apply output sharpening after downscaling?

Yes. Downscaling algorithms naturally soften high-frequency edges. Applying a subtle unsharp mask (Radius: 0.5px, Amount: 30%) restores crisp micro-contrast before final encoding.

### What is the target file size for a blog post featured image?

A 1200×675px WebP featured image should weigh between 60KB and 100KB, delivering instant loading without visible compression artifacts.
