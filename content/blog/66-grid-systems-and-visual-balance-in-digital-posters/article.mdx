## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a modular grid in poster design?

A modular grid divides the canvas into both vertical columns and horizontal rows, creating an array of rectangular modules. Elements snap to module boundaries, guaranteeing structural harmony across headlines, photos, and captions.

### What is asymmetrical visual balance?

Asymmetrical balance achieves equilibrium between elements of differing visual weights—such as balancing a large, dark photograph on the left with a small, high-contrast, vibrantly colored headline block on the right.

### Can breaking the grid make a poster better?

Yes. Once a strong grid foundation is established, deliberately having a single element (such as a model's arm or a bold letterform) break through the grid boundary creates dramatic dynamism and energy.
