> **Summary:** Stop blending into search results. Discover how frame occupancy, dynamic 3D angles, and micro-contrast make your product thumbnails impossible to ignore.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is 'Frame Occupancy' and why does it drive clicks?

Frame occupancy is the percentage of total canvas area filled by the product. Products that fill 85% to 90% of the thumbnail appear larger, closer, and more detailed in mobile search results than timid competitors occupying only 50% of the box.

### Can I use props or badges on Amazon main images?

No. Amazon strictly bans props, non-included accessories, watermarks, and promotional badges (like 'Sale' or 'Best Seller') on the main image. Violating this will trigger algorithmic search suppression.

### How does Click-Through Rate (CTR) affect Amazon A9/Cosmo search rank?

CTR is a primary ranking factor. If 100 shoppers search 'wireless earbuds' and 35 click your listing while only 10 click a competitor, Amazon's algorithm instantly promotes your listing higher because it generates more revenue.
