## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do some online converters turn transparent backgrounds black?

Inferior converters flatten images onto a canvas without alpha channel initialization or discard the alpha channel during lossy RGB quantization. FluxWebP preserves the full 8-bit alpha layer natively.
