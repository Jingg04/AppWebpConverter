# What is EXIF Metadata? GPS Location, Camera Settings, and Privacy Risks

<VisualAsset id="va-91-1" type="hero" src="/public/blog-assets/91-what-is-exif-metadata-gps-location-privacy-risks/hero.svg" alt="3D visual of photograph peeling open to reveal hidden EXIF metadata and GPS satellite coordinates" />

> **Summary:** Every photo you snap records where you were standing. Explore the privacy risks of EXIF metadata: embedded GPS coordinates, timestamps, and device fingerprints.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What exact information does EXIF data contain?

Exchangeable Image File Format (EXIF) stores camera make/model, lens specifications, focal length, exposure settings (ISO, shutter speed, aperture), exact date/timestamp, device unique serial number, and exact GPS latitude, longitude, and altitude.

### Do platforms like Twitter, Instagram, and Reddit remove EXIF data?

Most major social networks strip EXIF data when re-encoding photos for feeds. However, sharing photos via email attachments, cloud drives, SMS/iMessage, forums, personal blogs, or classified ads (Craigslist) often preserves raw EXIF, leaking your exact location.

### How accurate is the GPS location in a smartphone photo?

Modern smartphones combine GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, and cellular triangulation to record coordinates accurate to within 3 to 5 meters (10 to 15 feet)—enough to pinpoint an exact apartment balcony or front door.
