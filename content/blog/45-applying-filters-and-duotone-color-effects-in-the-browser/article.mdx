## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a duotone effect?

A duotone is a photographic reproduction technique that replaces the full color spectrum with exactly two colors: one dark tint for shadows (e.g. deep navy or midnight blue) and one light tint for highlights (e.g. neon yellow, pink, or mint green).

### How is duotone calculated in code?

The image is first converted to grayscale luminosity (Y = 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B). The resulting 0.0 to 1.0 luminosity value is used as an interpolation factor to blend between Color A (shadows) and Color B (highlights).

### Why are duotone images so popular in digital branding?

Duotones unify disparate, mismatched stock photos into a consistent, branded visual language while providing exceptional high-contrast backdrops for readable typography.
