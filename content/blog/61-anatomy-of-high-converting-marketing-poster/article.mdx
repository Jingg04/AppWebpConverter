## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the '3-Second Rule' in poster design?

A passerby or social media scroller decides within 3 seconds whether your poster is worth their attention. In those 3 seconds, your poster must communicate: 1. What it is, 2. Why they should care, and 3. Where/When it happens.

### What is the Z-pattern and F-pattern reading flow?

Western readers scan visual media in a 'Z' path: starting at the top-left (logo/date), sweeping across to the top-right, cutting diagonally down through the center hero image, and finishing at the bottom-right (Call to Action).

### What is the ideal ratio between image, text, and white space?

A time-tested marketing formula is: 50% Visual Imagery, 30% Typographic Hierarchy, and 20% Negative/White Space for breathing room.
