> **Summary:** Stop creating disjointed, random marketing graphics. Discover how to anchor AI art styles, typography tokens, and layout grids into a unified brand identity system.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you keep AI art looking consistent across 10 different posters?

Lock three variables in your prompt pipeline: 1. The Art Medium (e.g. 'Matte ceramic 3D clay render'), 2. The Color Palette Tokens (e.g. 'Deep obsidian #0A0A0A and electric violet #8B5CF6'), and 3. The Lighting Model (e.g. 'Soft 45-degree diffused studio spotlight').

### What belongs in a digital brand kit?

A complete digital brand kit includes: primary and secondary logo marks (SVG/PNG), primary and secondary font families, a 5-color hex palette, fixed margin/padding rules, and reusable master poster templates.

### Can marketing teams share brand templates in FluxWebP?

Yes. Brand templates can be saved locally or exported as a lightweight JSON configuration containing all layer geometries, font pairings, and color tokens.
