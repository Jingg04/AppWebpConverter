## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the standard container width for email templates?

600px is the universal standard container width across email clients (Gmail, Apple Mail, Outlook) to ensure emails fit neatly in desktop reading panes without horizontal scrolling.

### Why should I export email images at 1200px width?

Most email is read on smartphones and Retina laptops with 2x pixel density. Exporting at 1200px width and displaying via HTML as width='600' ensures razor-sharp text and graphics on high-DPI screens.

### Why does Outlook display images at 200% size?

Desktop Outlook on Windows uses the Word rendering engine. If an image lacks explicit HTML width attributes, Outlook reads the Windows system DPI (e.g. 125% or 150%) and blows the image out of its container.
