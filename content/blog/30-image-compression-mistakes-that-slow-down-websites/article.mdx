> **Summary:** Avoid the 10 most common image optimization mistakes: over-compression, missing dimensions, unresized uploads, and improper formats that destroy web speed.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the single most common image mistake on business websites?

Uploading raw, unresized photos directly from smartphones or DSLR cameras (e.g. 5000px wide, 8 MB) and letting CSS shrink them to fit a 400px blog column.
