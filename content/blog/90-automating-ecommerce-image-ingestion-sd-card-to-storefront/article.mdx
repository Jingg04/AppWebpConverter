## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much time does automated image ingestion save?

Manual photo preparation (renaming, background removal, cropping, color correction, WebP compression, and uploading) averages 12 to 15 minutes per product. Automated pipelines reduce this to under 15 seconds per SKU.

### How does barcode or SKU scanning connect photos to products?

At the start of each product shoot, the photographer snaps a quick photo of the barcode tag. The ingestion script uses optical barcode recognition to automatically extract the SKU and name all subsequent photos accordingly.

### Can FluxWebP automate this entire pipeline client-side?

Yes. Using FluxWebP batch tools and browser file system APIs, you can drop a folder of raw camera files, and the browser will automatically crop, remove backgrounds, convert to sRGB, compress to WebP, and bundle an organized ZIP.
