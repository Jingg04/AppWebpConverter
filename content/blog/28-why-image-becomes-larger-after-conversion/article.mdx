# Why an Image Can Become Larger After Conversion

> **Summary:** Ever converted a JPEG to WebP and ended up with a larger file? Understand the compression paradox, noise amplification, and how to prevent file size inflation.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is my converted WebP larger than my original JPEG?

If your input JPEG was already heavily compressed (e.g. at 50% quality), converting it to WebP at a higher quality setting (e.g. 85%) forces the WebP encoder to preserve and mathematically describe the existing JPEG compression artifacts as 'real detail', resulting in a larger file.
