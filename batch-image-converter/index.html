There are no artificial software limits or daily upload quotas. Because encoding runs locally on your computer hardware, you can batch convert dozens or hundreds of photos without subscription paywalls.

Yes, clicking 'Upload Folder' lets you select an entire folder structure. FluxWebP Pro recursively filters all compatible image assets.
