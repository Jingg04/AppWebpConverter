Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Zero Remote Server Uploads

FluxWebP Pro operates strictly as a client-side web application. When you drag and drop or select files to convert, your images are decoded and encoded directly within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly, and local Web Worker threads. At no point are your image files, file names, or image metadata uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud.

2. Location & EXIF Metadata Sanitization

By default, FluxWebP Pro strips GPS latitude and longitude tags from all exported files to protect your physical location privacy when publishing photos online. You have full control in the Advanced Settings drawer to retain or strip EXIF metadata.

3. Local Browser Storage & Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels. Your preferences (e.g. chosen quality level, dark/light theme, and local conversion history) are saved exclusively in your browser's localStorage on your own device. You can purge all saved records at any time via Application Settings.

4. Privacy-First Analytics

Any performance metric tracking is strictly anonymous, client-side, and aggregated (e.g., counting total conversions completed locally). We never transmit file contents or identifiable personal information.
