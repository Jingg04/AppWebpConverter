Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

Summary: FluxWebP Pro processes your photos locally inside your browser sandbox by default. We do not store, view, or sell your files. When you explicitly use cloud-based AI generation in the Poster Studio, prompt text and selected reference imagery are transmitted securely to designated AI model inference endpoints.

1. Local On-Device Processing (Default Engine)

The primary tools in FluxWebP Pro—including the Universal Image Converter, Image Compressor, Image Resizer, Image Editor, and the Fast Saliency Background Remover—operate 100% on your device. File decoding, raster transformations, WebAssembly operations, and format encoding (WebP, PNG, JPEG, AVIF) occur strictly inside your browser's V8 engine and Web Worker threads. Your source images never touch our servers.

2. AI Poster Studio & Cloud Inference Disclosure

When using the AI Poster Studio (`/poster-studio/`), you can generate original creative artwork from text prompts. These generations utilize serverless inference connections:

  • FLUX.1 Cloud Engine (Default): Generates visuals based on your text prompt via public inference endpoints without requiring user accounts or storing personal files.
  • Google Imagen 3 & OpenAI DALL-E 3 (Optional BYOK): When you configure a private API key, requests are routed directly to Google or OpenAI over encrypted TLS connections.
  • Reference Images: When you upload a product photo for AI compositing, background segmentation is performed client-side on your device first. Only user-approved generation queries are sent externally.

3. Metadata Sanitization & EXIF Stripping

To protect your real-world physical location privacy, FluxWebP Pro strips GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude) by default from all converted images. In the Advanced Settings drawer, you can customize whether camera EXIF metadata (lens, aperture, shutter speed) is retained or stripped.

4. Local Browser Storage (LocalStorage)

FluxWebP Pro stores project drafts (.fluxproj and .fluxposter documents), Brand Kits, and conversion history strictly on your device using HTML5 localStorage and IndexedDB. We do not maintain server-side user accounts or cross-device cloud databases. You can permanently wipe all locally cached data at any time by clicking "Purge All Saved Data" in Settings.

5. Cookies and Analytics

We do not use advertising tracking pixels or commercial tracking cookies. Diagnostic metrics (such as page response latency and error status codes) are collected in aggregated, anonymized form to monitor system uptime.

6. User Rights & Contact

Because we do not store your files or identifiable account profiles on remote servers, your right to erase, inspect, or retrieve your data is instantaneously exercisable on your machine through your browser settings or our built-in purge utility. For questions, reach out via our Contact Page.
